How the pipeline works

This is the architecture behind every ticket — for what happened to one specific ticket, see the workflow activity feed below.

StageWhat it doesPowered by
1. Pre-processing Lowercases text, strips URLs and boilerplate greetings/sign-offs that appear in almost every ticket regardless of category Regex, classifier.py
2. Classification Predicts department (category) and urgency (severity) from the cleaned text TF-IDF + Logistic Regression
3. Priority calculation Combines severity + business impact into a P1–P4 priority Rule-based lookup
4. KB retrieval Finds the 2-3 most relevant knowledge-base articles for this specific ticket TF-IDF + cosine similarity
5. Resolution generation Extracts the real numbered steps from the retrieved articles and cites the source of each one Rule-based extraction, rag_pipeline.py
6. Validation Scores overall confidence and decides AUTO_RESOLVE vs ESCALATE Weighted formula, agents.py
No external AI API is called anywhere in this pipeline — every stage runs locally on this server. Steps 2 and 4 are separate ML models with separate accuracy numbers, measured below.
